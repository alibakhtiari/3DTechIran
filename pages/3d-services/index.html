3D Engineering & Design Services

End-to-End Solutions from Concept to High-Precision Manufacturing

3D TECHIRAN provides a complete suite of specialized 3D digital manufacturing services designed to accelerate product development cycles, elevate component quality, and reduce fabrication overhead. Our engineering team combines deep domain expertise in industrial design, mold manufacturing, and reverse engineering with cutting-edge optical digitizing and additive manufacturing systems.

Our Core Capabilities

1. High-Precision 3D Optical Scanning

Non-contact optical and structured-light 3D scanning with micron-level accuracy. We capture dense point clouds and high-fidelity polygonal meshes of complex geometries, freeform surfaces, molds, tooling, and intricate mechanical assemblies.

2. Full Reverse Engineering (RE)

Transforming physical parts and damaged tooling into fully parametric native CAD models (SolidWorks, CATIA, NX, STEP, IGES). We optimize geometries, eliminate manufacturing defects, and generate complete 2D manufacturing drawings.

3. Industrial 3D Printing & Prototyping

Rapid fabrication of functional prototypes and end-use components using FDM, SLA, DLP, and SLS technologies with engineering-grade thermoplastics, high-temperature resins, and composite materials.

4. Mold, Tooling & Bottle Design

Specialized design and engineering of blow molds, injection molds, packaging, and custom glass bottles for the beverage, pharmaceutical, cosmetics, and food packaging sectors.
